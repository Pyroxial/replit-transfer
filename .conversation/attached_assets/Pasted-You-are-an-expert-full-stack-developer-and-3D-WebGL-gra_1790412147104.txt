### PART 1: UNDERSTANDING THE PRODUCT & MECHANICS (CONTEXT)
The BBREU is a bio-resorbable 3D-printed sleeve that bridges a missing bone section (e.g., human femur) without permanent metal screws, plates, or external steel frames.

1. THE ANATOMY & LAYER ARCHITECTURE (4 CONCENTRIC LAYERS):
   - Layer 0 (Core Graft): A central 7+ cm live vascularized fibula graft (with a red pulsating artery/vein) surrounded by bone marrow aspirate (BMAC gel).
   - Layer 1 (Innermost Engine): High concentration of Agent A (Methacrylated Hyaluronic Acid - MA-HA Hydrogel). Absorbs fluid rapidly, swells to exert INWARD RADIAL COMPRESSION onto the graft, and degrades fast (Months 1–3) to make room for new bone.
   - Layer 2 (Middle Structural Shell): High concentration of Agent B (PLLA-PCL composite with Magnesium Whiskers). Rigid, slow-degrading (Months 12–18). Acts as an unyielding structural anvil that forces swelling inward while bearing heavy weight loads.
   - Layer 3 (Outermost Hydrophobic Seal Skin): An ultra-thin, non-porous hydrophobic sealant skin wrapping the exterior perimeter. It acts as an impermeable fluid barrier preventing surrounding muscle fluids from eroding the casing from the outside, ensuring strict INSIDE-OUT degradation.
   - Mechanical Ends: Top and bottom ends of the BBREU sleeve feature custom 3D jigsaw-style interlocking step-bevel cuts that lock directly into the host bone ends to resist torsional shear without metal screws.

2. INSIDE-OUT DIFFERENTIAL DEGRADATION:
   - Fluid can only enter through the internal core.
   - The inner hydrogel layer (Layer 1) absorbs fluid and degrades first while squeezing the graft.
   - As new bone consolidates, the outer structural shell (Layer 2) slowly erodes.
   - The ultra-thin outer protective skin (Layer 3) stays intact for 12+ months, breaking down last so zero external fluid degrades the unit early.
   - Result: 100% natural host bone remains with zero hardware left behind.
